# Design System: IELTS Training Workbench

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Annotated Test-Room Track"**

The interface is a four-surface pocket control desk. Today establishes time and daily completion, Training keeps four IELTS skills in one continuous track, AI Feedback turns a submitted answer into an actionable critique, and Library holds questions, model answers, and imported material. The four tabs close the loop between planning, practice, feedback, and evidence.

The world is code-led and flat. Deep cobalt fields, cool paper, chartreuse attention wash, drawn icons, and thin rules do the structural work; shadows and ornament do not. AI results use the same paper inset and rule language as practice records, while the Library uses compact tab pills and ruled cards rather than a separate browsing style.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Deep cobalt for timekeeping and navigation, white or cool paper for practice, and chartreuse for the active round.
- A persistent four-tab shell for Today, Training, AI Feedback, and Library; the mobile dock becomes a sticky desktop strip without changing its order.
- One continuous skill track instead of four equal dashboard cards.
- A flat workbench grammar shared by timers, forms, feedback, library cards, and evidence rows.
- Thin rules, square-ish controls, and a single 1.8-stroke drawn icon family.
- Condensed tabular numerals for every important duration and score.

## Colors

The palette behaves like a marked exam timetable: a dark working rail, pale paper for study records, one highlighter signal, and fixed color identities for the four skills. Inverse support tones stay inside the cobalt family rather than introducing a second dark palette.

### Primary

- **Exam Cobalt** (`rail`): The timekeeping field, desktop exam rail, and the page's dominant contrast surface.
- **Midnight Cobalt** (`rail-deep`): The fixed top bar and darkest perimeter tone.
- **Timer Chamber** (`timer-field`): The inset focus-timer background, darker than the rail so the active session reads as a working compartment.

### Secondary

- **Highlighter Chartreuse** (`accent`): Active-lane wash, progress, active tab, primary start action, target badge, and the top rule.
- **Highlighter Ink** (`accent-ink`): Text and icons on chartreuse action surfaces.

### Tertiary

- **Record Blue** (`blue`): Practice logging, evidence logging, focus borders on light fields, and the reading skill.
- **Listening Amber**, **Writing Rose**, and **Speaking Green** (`listening`, `writing`, `speaking`): Fixed skill identities used across lane icons, latest-band dots, and history chips.
- **Error Rose** (`danger`): Destructive delete feedback only.

### Neutral

- **Cool Paper** (`paper`): The page field, form wells, feedback result inset, file import field, and model-answer well.
- **Evidence White** (`surface`): Panels, library cards, fields, buttons, and high-contrast type surfaces.
- **Schedule Ink** (`ink`): Primary text, the bottom navigation shell, active library filter, and toast surface.
- **Pencil Muted** (`muted`): Supporting copy, field labels, durations, record notes, and empty-state text.
- **Rule Light** and **Rule Strong** (`line`, `line-strong`): Dividers, field borders, track rules, and card outlines.
- **Focus Blue** (`focus`): The global keyboard focus outline.
- **Rail Faint** (`rail-faint`): Quiet subheads on cobalt. **Rail Copy** (`rail-copy`) and **Topbar Muted** (`topbar-muted`) carry explanatory and date copy without competing with the countdown.
- **Nav Muted** (`nav-muted`): Inactive labels in the four-tab navigation.
- **Timer Muted** and **Timer Copy** (`timer-muted`, `timer-copy`): Timer labels, module metadata, and coaching footer on the dark chamber.
- **Control Muted** (`control-muted`) and **Danger Wash** (`danger-wash`): Quiet delete controls and their restrained destructive hover state.
- **Cue Wash** (`cue-wash`): The next-round reminder surface, a pale companion to the chartreuse active wash.

### Named Rules

**The Cobalt Ground Rule.** Cobalt owns time and navigation; never spread it across the whole practice field or turn it into decorative gradients.

**The Highlighter Signal Rule.** Chartreuse marks active work, progress, or the next action; it is not ambient decoration.

**The Skill Color Rule.** Each skill keeps one fixed hue across its lane icon, latest-band dot, and record chip.

## Typography

**Display Font:** ZCOOL QingKe HuangYou (with Barlow Condensed and Microsoft YaHei UI fallbacks)  
**Body Font:** Segoe UI Variable Text (with Aptos and Segoe UI fallbacks)  
**Numeric / Label Font:** Barlow Condensed for countdowns and scores; Segoe UI Variable Text for compact labels and controls

**Character:** The pairing is a sharp editorial prompt laid over a practical instrument panel. The display face carries Chinese-language personality and wide visual cuts, while the body and condensed numerals stay immediate and operational across forms, records, and AI feedback.

### Hierarchy

- **Display** (400, clamp(2.45rem, 5.6vw, 5rem), 0.96): Large training questions and section statements.
- **Page Title** (400, clamp(2.1rem, 5vw, 4.4rem), 0.98): AI Feedback and Library introductions; **Display Compact** and **Display Mobile** step down to clamp(2rem, 8.8vw, 3.4rem) and clamp(2rem, 9vw, 3.1rem) when the page narrows.
- **Countdown** (650, clamp(4.4rem, 7vw, 6rem), 0.78): Exam-day count; it becomes a direct 3.6rem step on phones.
- **Timer** (620, clamp(4rem, 7vw, 6rem), 0.82): Active-round remaining time; phones use clamp(3.8rem, 20vw, 4.8rem).
- **Section Title, Lane Title, and Status** (700-750, 1.05-1.25rem): Panel headings, skill names, and timer state.
- **Body** (400, 16px, 1.5): Instructions, form values, task names, and records; explanatory copy stays within roughly 68 characters per line.
- **Compact Reading** (400, 0.9-0.92rem): Model answers use 0.9rem with 1.75 line height; feedback, timers, and lane copy use 0.91-0.92rem.
- **Small Support** (400, 0.82-0.88rem): Captions, record notes, evidence detail, and result body copy.
- **Control and Metadata** (700-800, 0.76-0.8rem): Footnote text, dates, durations, library tabs, and compact control copy.
- **Label and Micro-label** (700-800, 0.58-0.72rem): Uppercase field labels, lane metadata, and the phone completion label.

### Named Rules

**The Two-Voice Rule.** Use the display face for major questions and statements only; controls, notes, records, and feedback stay in the UI sans.

**The Timescale Rule.** Durations and scores use condensed tabular numerals; never let display lettering compete with a running clock.

**The Compact Instrument Rule.** Metadata, feedback, and library controls stay in the compact sans ramp; sitting inside a panel does not promote them to display type.

## Layout

The product is a four-surface application inside one fixed frame. A midnight top bar remains present, while the navigation uses a fixed bottom dock below 720px and a sticky strip below the top bar at wider widths. Today stacks its exam-control panel and evidence chapter; Training keeps the four lanes continuous; AI Feedback uses a two-column form/result workspace; Library uses horizontal tabs, filter chips, and a ruled card list.

On wide screens, Today places the exam information in a `minmax(0, 1.15fr) / minmax(280px, .85fr)` grid with 28px by 42px gaps. The layout stacks at 900px. At 720px the clock condenses to countdown, setup fields, and daily completion, while secondary band and coaching detail drop out. The application remains usable down to 390px.

Training is ruled into four continuous lanes. The active lane expands from `auto 0fr` into `auto 1fr`, revealing the timer in place while the other lanes remain visible. AI Feedback uses a `1.05fr / .95fr` two-column split with 24px separation and stacks at 900px. The evidence ledger keeps a wider practice panel and narrower evidence panel until the existing 1100px stack point.

Spacing is a dense instrument rhythm rather than a generous marketing scale: 4-8px for metadata and icon internals, 12-18px for form and card groups, 24px for panel separation, 28-42px for Today's major grid, 38px for ledger starts, and 48-86px before evidence chapters. Page gutters use clamp(22px, 4.5vw, 64px), so the same composition breathes without changing reading order.

### Named Rules

**The Four-Surface Rule.** Today, Training, AI Feedback, and Library each own one job; do not collapse them into a generic dashboard grid or detach navigation from the ordered tab shell.

**The Continuous Track Rule.** Skills are stacked lanes separated by rules, never four equal independent cards.

**The Expanded Lane Rule.** Selection expands the chosen lane inline and keeps the other skills in view; do not replace the track with a modal or a separate timer screen.

**The Reading-Order Rule.** Countdown first, active practice second, AI or library material third, and evidence last.

## Elevation & Depth

This system is flat by default. Depth comes from four mechanisms: cobalt fields against cool paper, tonal inset wells, 1px ruled separators, and overlapping or concentric circular geometry. Panels, buttons, navigation, AI results, and library cards do not float on shadows.

### Shadow Vocabulary

- **Focus Halo** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(72, 100, 232, 0.13)`): Attached to focused light fields to reinforce the blue border change.
- **Exam Orbit Inner** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 36px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.025)`): The first quiet ring around the exam-clock circle.
- **Exam Orbit Outer** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 72px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.018)`): The second, fainter structural ring.

### Named Rules

**The Flat Workbench Rule.** Surfaces are flat at rest; state is expressed through color, rule weight, position, and the focus halo, not card elevation.

**The Anchored Dock Rule.** The four-tab navigation is a solid ink strip with a rule border; it never becomes a floating glass capsule.

**The Structural Ring Rule.** Circular outlines belong to the exam-clock geometry and timer-chamber ornament; they are not a general container treatment.

## Shapes

The form language is square-ish and administrative. Small indicators use 2px corners, checkboxes 7px, fields and buttons 9-10px, navigation items and feedback results 11px, panels and cards 12-14px, and the navigation shell 16px. Only status and selection objects become fully pill-shaped: target badges, duration presets, module chips, library tabs and filters, and progress tracks.

Borders are precise and thin. The training track relies on 1px rules, controls use a single border, and dashed rules are reserved for empty evidence states and the file import field. Icons are drawn as one family with rounded caps and joins, a 1.8 stroke, and a 24px source grid. Circle motifs appear only as large concentric exam-clock or timer-chamber geometry.

### Named Rules

**The Square-ish Utility Rule.** Controls, panels, and state boxes use restrained radii; cards never become capsules.

**The Pill Exception Rule.** Pills are allowed only for badges, chips, library tabs, filters, preset durations, and progress tracks where their enclosure communicates selection or quantity.

## Components

### Buttons

- **Shape:** Softly squared controls (10px radius) with a 44px minimum height.
- **Primary:** Chartreuse fill with highlighter ink, a 1.8-stroke play icon, and 10px 16px padding; used to start or pause the active round.
- **Record:** Solid record blue with white text and the same shape; used for practice records, evidence entries, AI submission, and import submission.
- **Secondary:** Evidence-white fill with rule-strong border; used for copying feedback and saving it to the evidence ledger.
- **Quiet:** Transparent with a translucent white border on cobalt; used for timer reset.
- **Hover / Focus / Active:** Primary and record buttons lighten or shift, secondary actions gain a blue border, all controls receive the 3px blue focus outline, and pressing any control settles by 1px.

### Fields

- **Light Field:** White fill, rule-strong border, 9px radius, 44px minimum height, and ink text. Focus changes the border to record blue and adds the focus halo.
- **Dark Field:** Translucent white fill, 22% white border, 10px radius, and white text on cobalt. Its label stays uppercase and muted; focus changes only the border to chartreuse.
- **AI Textareas:** The question field is at least 140px high; the submitted answer field is at least 260px high. Both keep the light-field grammar and vertical resizing.
- **File Import:** A dashed rule-strong outline on cool paper, 10px radius, and 12px padding; it belongs to the Library import panel, not to the navigation or AI form.

### Chips and Badges

- **Style:** Pill-shaped, compact, and derived from a single module color through a light white mix.
- **Module Identity:** A 7px square marker precedes the label in record chips; skill rows use the raw module hue for the corresponding band dot or lane icon.
- **Target Badge:** Chartreuse text and border on cobalt; it names the target without competing with the countdown.
- **Library Tabs and Filters:** 38px-high pills. Inactive items are paper-white with a rule border; the active item becomes solid ink with white text, so selection is legible without another accent color.

### Cards and Containers

- **Corner Style:** 14px for major panels and the timer chamber; 12px for AI/library cards, record wells, and empty states; 11px for feedback results and navigation items.
- **Background:** Evidence-white panels and library cards on cool paper; the active lane uses a pale chartreuse wash, the timer uses timer-chamber cobalt, and AI results use cool paper.
- **Shadow Strategy:** Flat; see Elevation & Depth.
- **Border:** One rule-light stroke for panels and library cards, rule-strong for the track and interactive fields, and dashed rule-strong for empty states and import.
- **Internal Padding:** 20-30px for panels, 14-18px for form wells and library cards, 17px for AI results, and 18-28px for the timer depending on viewport.

### Navigation

- **Top Bar:** A sticky midnight-cobalt bar with a 2px chartreuse rule that draws left-to-right on entry. Its four-square skill mark rotates slightly and carries the four fixed module hues.
- **Four-Tab Shell:** Solid Schedule Ink with four equal icon-and-label items. Today, Training, AI Feedback, and Library retain their order; the active item is chartreuse with highlighter ink.
- **Mobile:** Fixed 8-12px from the viewport edges, 16px outer radius, 52px minimum item height, and safe-area-inset padding. The top bar drops its date and English descriptor below 720px.
- **Desktop:** The same dock becomes sticky below the top bar, centered at up to 560px wide, rather than turning into a sidebar.

### AI Feedback Workspace

The AI surface pairs an evidence-white form panel with a result panel. The form stacks mode, question, answer, optional API key, a privacy checkbox, and the record-blue submit action with 18px separation. The result is a cool-paper `pre` inset with a 310px minimum height, 17px padding, 11px radius, and scrolling overflow; copy and evidence actions sit below it. Saved feedback appears as compact history rows with rule-light borders and 9px corners. The result never becomes a chat transcript or a second accent-colored surface.

### Library Workspace

The Library uses three horizontal tabs: questions, model answers, and import. Question and model-answer views use a single-column card list with 12px gaps, module metadata above the title, and evidence-white cards with 18px padding and 12px corners. Model answers reveal a paper inset at 0.9rem with 1.75 line height, followed by annotation rules. Import combines the standard panel form with a dashed file field for `.txt`, `.md`, and `.json` files.

### Training Lane / Signature Component

Each skill is a full-width ruled row with a colored 46px icon tile, Chinese and English name, editable task summary, duration, disclosure control, and separate completion affordance. The active lane receives the chartreuse wash, expands its timer chamber inline, and rotates only its disclosure control. Completed lanes keep their location but strike through the task label. On mobile the icon and task occupy the first row, duration and disclosure the second, and the completion control stays at the right edge.

### Timer Chamber / Signature Component

The timer is inset inside the active lane rather than presented as a detached dashboard card. It carries the module marker, round status, editable task field, condensed countdown, primary start/pause action, pill duration presets, a chartreuse progress track, and a two-line coaching footer. Its entrance uses a clipped reveal with a 10px rise; reduced-motion mode removes the effect.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** keep cobalt for time and navigation, cool paper for practice and feedback, and chartreuse for the active round.
- **Do** keep the four-tab order and make the active tab a chartreuse solid with highlighter ink.
- **Do** use the continuous lane track and expand the timer inside the selected lane.
- **Do** preserve fixed hues for listening, reading, writing, and speaking across every repeated marker.
- **Do** keep AI results in a cool-paper inset and library content in ruled evidence-white cards.
- **Do** use thin rules, inset tonal wells, and tabular numerals to create structure and precision.
- **Do** keep pills limited to badges, chips, library tabs, filters, preset durations, and progress tracks.
- **Do** retain the single 1.8-stroke drawn icon family and 44px minimum interactive control height.

### Don't:

- **Don't** replace the lane track with equal-card skill grids, ring metrics, or a generic dashboard shell.
- **Don't** turn the four-tab navigation into a sidebar, a glass dock, or a set of elevated floating buttons.
- **Don't** turn AI feedback into a generic chat transcript or add a second decorative accent to the result.
- **Don't** add gradients, glass effects, or soft floating-card shadows.
- **Don't** round panels into capsules or use pills as ordinary buttons.
- **Don't** use chartreuse as a broad background unrelated to active work or progress.
- **Don't** introduce raster decoration, mixed icon weights, or a second illustration style.
- **Don't** let decorative type compete with the countdown, timer, task copy, feedback, or form labels.
